About FEDPONAM Comrades Association

The FEDPONAM Comrades Association (FCA) is a lifelong network of former and current comrades of Federal Polytechnic Kaura Namoda. The Association promotes unity, leadership, welfare, mentorship, institutional development, and service to humanity.

Vision

To become the leading comrades' association that empowers members and contributes meaningfully to the growth of Federal Polytechnic Kaura Namoda and society.

Mission

To unite comrades, provide support, promote leadership, encourage professional development, and uphold the ideals of justice, solidarity, and progress.
