About FCA

Who We Are

The FedPoNaM Comrades Association (FCA) is an association created to bring together graduates and comrades of Federal Polytechnic Kaura Namoda.

FCA promotes unity, networking, leadership, professional development, welfare and meaningful contributions to society.
